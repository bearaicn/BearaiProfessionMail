## 相较v0.3.2的更新

- 新增时光服兼容、三客户端独立数据域及按构建生成的本地专业材料索引。数据来自wago.tools的SkillLineAbility与SpellReagents，2026-11-04更新，基于era1.15.9.70576、forever1.60.1.70755、titan3.80.2.70522；来源、覆盖与限制见README和data-sources文档。
- 独立物品规则面板支持拖入、用户覆盖、未收录物品维护；专业用途、游戏物品类别、名称／ID共同筛选。
- 邮箱入口切到发件箱、三行33格、具体专业一键添加；双击选择数量，拆分解锁后加入原生附件。
- 右键按具体堆叠添加／撤回，修复同名另一堆误撤回和锁定背包槽位重复格。水下诱鱼器6533仅归钓鱼用品。

已添加物品保留勾号，同时图标置灰；撤回后移除勾号，背包解锁后恢复颜色。

自动检查覆盖三客户端模拟、26个Lua文件语法、部署隔离和安装包校验。不能替代各客户端游戏验收。插件不会自动发送邮件；玩家必须核对原生收件人、附件和邮资后自行发送。

## 安装

按客户端选择一个ZIP，使用`SHA256SUMS-ALL.txt`校验；将其中的BearaiProfessionMail文件夹放入对应客户端Interface/AddOns。首次从旧版升级请完全退出游戏后重启。平台自动生成的源码ZIP不是精简安装包。

## English

Three packages share tag v0.5.6: Classic Era / Hardcore 1.15.9 (11509), Forever 1.60.1 (16001), and Titan 3.80.2 (38002). This is a prerelease. Era 0.3.2 was user-verified; the current Era build awaits regression. Forever remains experimental. The user confirmed the Titan stack right-click fix; the latest icon styling and full regression remain pending.

Since v0.3.2: Titan support and separate client profiles; build-specific profession indexes sourced from wago.tools SkillLineAbility / SpellReagents (2026-11-04); independent item rules and user overrides; combined profession/category/search filters; a 33-cell grid; profession-specific bulk attachment; quantity splitting with unlock handling; per-stack attachment tracking and gray checked icons. Players still send mail manually.

Choose the ZIP for your client from the table above and verify SHA256SUMS-ALL.txt. Extract its BearaiProfessionMail folder into the matching client Interface/AddOns and fully restart the game. Automated tests do not establish complete in-game compatibility. Licensed under MIT.
